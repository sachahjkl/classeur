# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The first layer: folders read on worker threads, the details view (name, date, type, size) sorted naturally, the
  filter, the selection, tabs, panels side by side, the command palette, the crumbs and the path typed, rename in place,
  new folder, the sidebar of places and volumes.
- Copy, cut and paste of files (a cut is a move for the other file managers too), the trash, delete for good, in a queue
  of jobs with their progress; a name taken asks what to do.
- The folders shown are watched: a change made by any program shows at once.
- The Graphite look: dense rows, square corners, bars and lines, striped rows.
